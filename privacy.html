Privacy Policy – clipfarm

Last updated: 16 November 2026

What the app does

clipfarm is a private, internal tool used only by its operators. It uploads video clips and metadata files that the operators produce themselves into a folder in the operators' own Google Drive.

Google user data

  • The app requests only the scope https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file. It can access only files and folders that the app created itself. It cannot read, list, change or delete any other files in a Google Drive.
  • No Google user data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.
  • The OAuth access token is stored only on the operators' own server, to upload files.

Other personal data

The app does not collect personal data from visitors or third parties and does not use cookies or tracking on this website.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Contact

Questions: open an issue in this repository on GitHub.
